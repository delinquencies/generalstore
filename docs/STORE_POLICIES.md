## Contact

Questions about an item, shipping, or an order? Email us at **[support email]**. For order questions, include your order number and the email you used at checkout. For a vintage piece, we’re happy to check a measurement or take a closer photo before you buy.

## Shipping

### Where we ship

We ship from Texas to the contiguous United States. Available shipping options and charges appear at checkout before you pay.

### When your order ships

In-stock orders usually leave our shop within **2–5 business days**. Business days exclude weekends and holidays. Carrier transit time starts after we hand off your order. We’ll email tracking when it ships.

If we can’t ship within the promised time, we’ll contact you with an updated estimate when available and give you the option to accept the delay or cancel for a full refund on the unshipped merchandise.

### Address changes and delivery problems

Email **[support email]** as soon as you notice a mistake in your address. Include your order number. We’ll check whether we can make the change before the package ships.

If tracking says your package was delivered and you can’t find it, check the delivery location and with anyone who may have accepted it. If it’s still missing, contact us with your order number and tracking information so we can help investigate.

## Returns and exchanges

### Changed your mind?

Email **[support email]** within **14 calendar days of delivery** to request a return for an eligible item. Include your order number and the item you want to return. Once we send instructions, mail it back within **14 calendar days**.

Return eligible goods unused, in the condition you received them, with original packaging and any included parts. Please pack them securely. You pay return shipping for change-of-mind returns. Original shipping charges are excluded from those refunds. We aim to issue approved refunds to the original payment method within **5 business days** of receiving the return. Your payment provider may take longer to post the refund.

### Final sale and exchanges

Vintage, secondhand, and one-of-a-kind pieces are final sale for change-of-mind returns **when marked Final Sale before purchase**. Opened personal-care products and consumables are also ineligible for change-of-mind returns. A SALE tag shows a discount. A 1/1 tag means there’s one of that piece. Look for a separate Final Sale notice on the listing.

If you want a different item, start a return and place a separate order. Availability can change, especially for vintage pieces.

### Damaged, wrong, or materially different from the listing

Contact us as soon as you can, ideally within **7 calendar days of delivery**. Include your order number and any photos you can share of the item and packaging. Keep the packaging while we work through the issue, if you still have it.

We’ll review what happened and arrange an appropriate replacement or refund. We cover the cost if we ask you to return an item that arrived damaged, was sent in error, or differs materially from the listing. Final Sale and change-of-mind limits don’t take away any rights you have for these problems. Wear clearly shown or described in a vintage listing is part of the item’s stated condition.

## How the draft was benchmarked

The shops reviewed aren’t a representative industry sample. General Store offers eligible returns for credit or exchange within 14 days, Ruby’s Old & New uses a 30-day window for eligible returns for credit or exchange, and Cousin offers 14-day refunds on eligible new goods. All three restrict vintage returns. The draft uses the common **14-day** window and offers an original-payment refund for eligible goods. [General Store](https://shop-generalstore.com/pages/return-policy) · [Ruby’s Old & New](https://rubysoldnew.com/pages/faq) · [Cousin](https://cousinst.com/pages/returns-exchanges)

General Store usually ships in 2–5 business days and Park & Oak Collected typically ships in-stock goods in 3–5. The draft proposes 2–5 days, subject to what Oddly Enough can reliably meet. [General Store shipping](https://shop-generalstore.com/pages/shipping) · [Park & Oak Collected FAQ](https://www.pocollected.com/pages/faq)
